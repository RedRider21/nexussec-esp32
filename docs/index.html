Cos'è (e cosa non è)What it is (and isn't)

La ESP32-DIV è un microcontrollore (ESP32-S3): non esegue Linux, ma unisce più radio in un solo dispositivo tascabile che la distro prepara e da cui riprende i dati.The ESP32-DIV is a microcontroller (ESP32-S3): it does not run Linux, but it combines several radios in one pocket device that the distro prepares and reads back.

✓ Fa✓ Does
WiFi: scan, sniffer, wardriving, deauth, beacon/probe spam, rogue AP / Evil Portal, PMKID/handshake. BLE: scan/spam. 2,4 GHz (NRF24): analisi e disturbo di canale. Sub-GHz (CC1101): cattura e replay di telecomandi (433/315/868/915 MHz). Infrarossi: cattura e replay. BadUSB/HID (ESP32-S3). WiFi: scan, sniffer, wardriving, deauth, beacon/probe spam, rogue AP / Evil Portal, PMKID/handshake. BLE: scan/spam. 2.4 GHz (NRF24): channel analysis and jamming. Sub-GHz (CC1101): capture and replay of remotes (433/315/868/915 MHz). Infrared: capture and replay. BadUSB/HID (ESP32-S3).
✗ Non fa✗ Doesn't
WiFi 5 GHz, monitor‑mode completo come una scheda vera, cracking pesante (quello lo fa il PC con i dati raccolti). 5 GHz WiFi, full monitor mode like a real card, heavy cracking (the PC does that with the collected data).
